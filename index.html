📖 Kua Merah Rules

Objective

Form 4 Pairs (8 cards total) in your hand before other players. Your winning payout depends on the red card point value of your winning hand!

Point Values

  • Red 10 (♥10, ♦10): 10 Points (Highest value card!)
  • Red 2 to 9: Face value (e.g. ♦7 = 7 Points)
  • Red J, Q, K: 2 Points each
  • Red Ace (♥A, ♦A): 1 Point
  • Black Cards (♠, ♣): 0 Points

Special Payouts (50 Base Points)

  • All Black Hand: 0 Red cards in winning hand.
  • Single Red Ace Hand: Only 1 Red Ace and 7 Black cards.
  • All Face Cards: 8 Court cards (J, Q, K).

Gameplay Rules

  • Drawing: On your turn, draw 1 card from the Middle Deck (secret) OR take the top card from the Discard Pile.
  • Same-Rank Discard Rule: If you take a card from the Discard Pile to form a pair, you cannot discard another card of that same rank on the exact same turn. You must wait at least 1 full round before discarding that rank again.
  • Out-of-Turn Stealing: If another player discards a card that completes 4 pairs for you, a prompt will ask if you want to steal and declare an instant win out of turn!
